# External asset provenance

Selected Ravenwood nature assets are sourced from the pinned `shorepine/kenney` mirror of Kenney's CC0 game assets.

Official Kenney support/license statement: https://kenney.nl/support

Selected source commit: `3694c6879e487c108f55677be7dd2ca75b07cc3b`

## KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0

Selected furniture assets are from KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0.

Repository: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0

Pinned source commit: `96d5930a8dbdb363409bbc2d3341718b00e17c9c`

The repository's `LICENSE.txt` identifies the pack as Creative Commons Zero (CC0), usable in commercial projects.

## Poly Haven

Approved for later Ravenwood PBR materials/HDRI sourcing. Poly Haven states all site assets are CC0.

License: https://polyhaven.com/license

## OpenGameArt mansion candidate

Asset: https://opengameart.org/content/3d-tileset-mansion

The asset page marks the mansion tileset CC0 and states that it includes a premade map. It remains a candidate until the downloaded archive receives geometry, scale and performance review.

## Policy

A link being free to download is not sufficient. Asset license, source, immutable revision where possible, and local file list must be recorded before integration.
